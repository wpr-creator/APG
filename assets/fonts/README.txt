APG self-hosted fonts

Sources: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/
Families: newsreader, publicsans, ibmplexmono
Downloaded November 13, 2026 from the official Google Fonts repository.
Converted from the original TTF files to WOFF2 with fontTools; glyph coverage retained.
Each family has its original SIL Open Font License included alongside the fonts.
Newsreader retains its variable optical-size and weight axes.
Public Sans retains its variable weight axis.
IBM Plex Mono includes regular, medium, and semibold.
Runtime: course-fonts.css with font-display:swap and existing fallback fonts.
