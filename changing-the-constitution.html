1.03 · CHANGING THE CONSTITUTION

CAN YOU CLEAR
THE FOUR GATES?

ARTICLE V created a formal process for amending the Constitution. It allowed the system to change, but required broad agreement so that temporary majorities could not easily rewrite the nation’s fundamental law.

01

THE BIG IDEA

CHANGE IS POSSIBLE.
CHANGE IS NOT EASY.

Article V separates an amendment into two jobs. First, the idea must be PROPOSED. Then the states must RATIFY, or formally approve, it.

AP CONNECTION

ADAPTABLE—BUT PROTECTED.

Article V balanced two competing goals. The Constitution had to remain adaptable, but its basic rules also needed protection from sudden political pressure.

TWO-THIRDS PROPOSED;
