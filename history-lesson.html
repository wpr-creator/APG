1.03 · FOUNDATIONS OF AMERICAN DEMOCRACY

THE HISTORY LESSON

ESSENTIAL QUESTION How did the United States replace a weak national government without creating another tyranny?

THE BIG IDEA

A STRONGER GOVERNMENT WITH LIMITS

The Constitution gave the national government enough power to do its job. It also divided and limited that power so no one part of government could control everything.
