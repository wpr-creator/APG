ESSENTIAL QUESTION

How did the United States replace a weak national government without creating another tyranny?

TYRANNY→WEAK GOVERNMENT→BALANCED POWER

THE HOOK

HOW MANY OF YOU HAVE STRICT PARENTS?

At home, they control your curfew, where you go, who you’re with, and how you spend your time. It can feel like every decision is made for you.

Now imagine moving away to SDSU. Suddenly, no one checks whether you went to class, tells you when to come home, or controls how you spend your money.

At first, total freedom feels amazing. Then you start missing assignments, running out of money, and heading toward academic probation.

THINK · PAIR · SHARE

Would you move back home and accept your parents’ rules—or what would you do?

THE BRIDGE

FROM ONE EXTREME TO ANOTHER

Total freedom sounded perfect—until having almost no rules created new problems. After independence, Americans also had to find a better balance.

ARTICLES OF CONFEDERATION

Protected the states from too much national control—but left the national government too weak.

CONSTITUTION AND RATIFICATION

Asked whether Americans could strengthen the government without sacrificing liberty.
