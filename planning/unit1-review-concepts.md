# Unit 1 review concepts

## Explain the Change in Power

Save for a future Unit 1 review activity. Do not display this on the History Lesson page.

### Causal chain

Fear of tyranny → weak national government → failures of the Articles of Confederation → stronger Constitution → ratification debate → Bill of Rights

### Possible review prompt

The United States replaced a weak national government by ________. It attempted to protect individual rights by ________. Ratification remained contested because ________.

The activity could ask students to arrange the six stages before completing the three-part explanation.
