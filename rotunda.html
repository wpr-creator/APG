    How to play

    Daily climb

    • Choose your units. The same unit selection gets the same daily prompts.
    • You have 25 seconds to name one thing that fits each prompt.
    • You get one scored submission per prompt. A pass or an expired timer earns no points.
    • Correct answers lift you higher. Less-used correct answers earn more leaderboard points.
    • Rarity points update as more students finish today’s climb: 20, 40, 60, 80, or 100 points per correct answer.
    • One daily attempt per unit selection on this device. A spelling suggestion does not pause the clock.

    Unit practice

    No timer. Use hints, retry answers, and explore the explanations. Practice does not enter the leaderboard.

    Daily scores for the selected units
