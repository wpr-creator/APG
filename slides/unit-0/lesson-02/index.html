COURSE, WORK & GRADES

AP U.S. Government and Politics is an introductory college-level course about the ideas, institutions, and political behavior that shape American government. We analyze foundational documents, Supreme Court cases, data, and current events from multiple perspectives while preparing for the AP Exam and informed civic life.
