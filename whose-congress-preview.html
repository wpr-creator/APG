01 / INVESTIGATE

The voters didn’t change.
The outcome did.

Teal has 60% of the voters in both maps. With horizontal districts, it wins three of five seats. With vertical districts, it wins all five.

That doesn’t tell us everything about a real map. But it reveals a powerful idea: drawing boundaries can shape representation before a single ballot is cast.

Who should get to draw the lines—and what should count as a good map?

ReapportionmentRedistributing House seats among states after the census.

RedistrictingDrawing the boundaries of districts within a state.

GerrymanderingDrawing districts to advantage a group or party.
